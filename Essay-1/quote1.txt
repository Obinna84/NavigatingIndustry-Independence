“If 3 companies, let's call them DistroKid, United Masters, and Tunecore. If all 3 of them have 97% of independent releases going through them in order to reach the market place, they could randomly for their own bottom line, raise the barriers to entry into the marketplace. They could make it to where it’s $100 a month to publish your music, or say ‘you got to give us 50% of your song revenue’ and there’s nothing illegal about doing that, so long as it’s in their terms of service. And as the independent artist with no leverage in the matter, we are asking for the access to these people’s aggregators. They’re not inviting us on. So whatever rules that they set, difficulties they assign to approaching music, we take those on.” - Mavi (Minder)
