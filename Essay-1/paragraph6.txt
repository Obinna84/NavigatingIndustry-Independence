These fears aren’t unfounded, and similar sentiments have been echoed by many other independent artists that have experienced being slighted by abrupt changes in terms of services, and difficulties resolving issues surrounding releases. Nevertheless the future need not be bleak. As a result of how recent this shift in the music industry has taken place, the clarity of information, and tools, independent artists can take advantage of are still ripe for the picking. While large aggregators may have a vested interest in circulating the idea that they’re the only avenue for independent artists to get their music onto DSPs, “At CD Baby we create the systems, artist tools, communication channels, and informational resources you need so you can upload your music ONCE and make it available worldwide in a successful way.”(CD Baby), there are many more players in the field willing to negotiate favorable terms to gain a competitive position in the market, and even options for artists to create their own aggregators (Minder).
