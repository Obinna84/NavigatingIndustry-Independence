Much progress has been made in the last twenty years, and the future of ownership for black artists looks brighter than it ever has before. Issues still exist when it comes to exploitation on the side of distribution with the current state of digital aggregators, but with more clarity on the information and tools available to creatives as time goes on these challenges may prove to not be long lasting. Hidden algorithms continue to drive more and more of our daily lives and interactions as staying offline becomes less of an option, however whether these forces will prove to be a net positive or negative in driving creative expression has yet to be fully shown. With many conclusions surrounding the future of the music industry still up in the air, one thing that seems to be certain is that the option of independence is here to stay, and young black artists will continue to be on the frontier of the pursuit of greater ownership over creative expression.
