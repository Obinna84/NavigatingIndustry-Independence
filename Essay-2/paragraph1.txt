In the ever-evolving music industry, having insight on its inner workings remains one of the most important tools for artists in gaining leverage, especially when it comes to retaining ownership over all facets of one’s brand. Thus, when sea changes like the switch from radio and physicals to digital streaming and distribution happen to flip the industry on its head, taking initiative on scouring for information can become life or death for an artist’s career. In this new era, music discovery is more democratized than it's ever been (Feyre 6), as a consequence of smarter social media algorithms, and every day more avenues to put out one’s music become available. Labels who served as gatekeepers to entrance into the music industry are becoming more obsolete in their traditional functions, and have now been forced to evolve in the roles they serve or double down on their insistence that they’re the sole lifeline for an artist to be successful.
