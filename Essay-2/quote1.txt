“It’s made it more intimate… as an independent artist you have a dedicated following of fans that listen to your music, and if you’re creating the vulnerable music that some of these creatives are making it’s almost like you’re giving the fans a little piece of you everytime you drop a song. And because of the different avenues of social media, and vlogging, and streaming that have just let people see more of the artist, people have a more intimate connection to the art and the artist.” - K Hamid (Kabia)
