The growing intimacy in artist-fan connection is not only beneficial in that it allows for greatly flexibility and visibility of creative expression, but it has also provided new avenues for artists to generate revenue from their music allowing for greater financial stability for independent artists and built truer direct-to-consumer pathways than those offered by aggregators. Websites that offer artists the ability to upload music and sell merchandise, set prices, and retain majority of the revenue have existed for a while, however innovations like those being made today by Vallejo artist LaRussell have the potential to make staying independent more viable than ever. With his brand Good Compenny, LaRussell has become a trailblazer in the pay what you want, or as they call it “proud to pay” model allowing for fans to put up offers to purchase releases, physicals (CDs and Vinyls) and concert tickets based on whatever they feel it’s worth, while still offering the option to stream his music on DSPs as well (Jeroslyn JoVonn). This pay-what-you-want model has seen growth in popularity amongst artists, recently being used by prolific rapper J Cole, on his Inevitable.live website for the release of his 2014 Forest Hills Drive [10 year anniversary edition] album starting at $10 for the digital edition (“Inevitable Audio Series: Jermaine Cole & Ibrahim Hamad”).
