Aggregators and digital distributors have effectively filled the shoes of delivering artists music to digital stores and streaming platforms (Robehmed), the now dominant mode of music consumption, and developed a market dominance sizable enough to alter the barriers of entry into music solely through changes in their terms of service (Minder). However, unlike it would seem on the surface level, large aggregators and dominant record labels of the past are not the only way to get one’s foot in the door. Innovations in direct to consumer models, as well as smaller and artist owned aggregators serve as a beacon of hope for artists seeking to retain ownership over their voice, catalogue, and brand. Alongside this new landscape of the music industry, comes the promise of a wealth of opportunities for success and independence for those willing to seek out the information available.
