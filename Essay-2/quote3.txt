“Do more market research to the ways you can deliver your music to the marketplace, because there’s so many companies that’ll do it for 0%, or 5%, or 10% and no monthly fees. It’s a competitive market even though it seems like there’s only a few major players, there’s so many companies trying to get their foot in the door. And especially with those companies you're able to have a more direct line of communication with their offices, and that’s one of the things that I’ve benefited from when they used to have Level Music. It was like a small office, a small team, I was able to go to there and talk to them about pitching, and how to make these things work the best… I think working with diverse companies, and not just the big names in the space, and having multiple revenue streams outside of DSPs in general is necessary for a healthy music career.” - MAVI (Minder)
