However, the ease of accessing information on alternative distribution options is where most of the difficulty comes from, and larger aggregators have a vested interest in ensuring this to maintain their market dominance. Moves like Spotify’s purchase of major stakes in DistroKid in 2018 (Dredge) are some of the more boding demonstrations of the growing power large aggregators wield in their proximity to DSPs. Due to how recent the development of the streaming era has been, the field of distribution is currently more akin to the Wild West than an oligopoly of major players. Finding how best to navigate distribution for one’s own brand, voice, and catalogue will likely be the next biggest hill for independent artists to climb in this new era.
