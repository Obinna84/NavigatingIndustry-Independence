Additionally, LaRussell recently introduced a stock system to his offer model (Sweet July), putting up a percentage of his royalties on a certain release for a fixed price that can be purchased by fans, and become passive income for them depending on how well the release sells . Not only does this model offer the opportunity for independent artists to create greater stability when starting out, but it also has the potential of deepening fan-artist relationship as both parties' success become intertwined. Due to the recency of these innovations the waters have yet to be tested on how expansive a model like this could go, but a fully integrated system that allows for any independent artist to sell shares of their royalties could prove to be a successful business model in the future.
