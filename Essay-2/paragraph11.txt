More So than ever before the option to become and remain an independent artist looks to be a viable pathway to stability through creative expression. Maintaining the benefits of flexibility in expressing one’s voice and persona that is inherent to independence, artists now also have the ability to garner audiences, and generate revenue that rivals that which would be possible under major label contracts of the past. As a result of newer technologies and the strength of social media algorithms, fan-artist relationships and interactions have also grown more intimate, making new direct-to-consumer models possible, decreasing the reliance independent artists have of DSPs and digital stores as sole revenue streams. For those willing to seek out information regarding favorable distribution options for their brand and voice, and put in the work of marketing themselves to receptive audiences, the developments of this era may hold the keys to greater stability, longevity, and success.
