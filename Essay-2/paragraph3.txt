While the adoption of aggregators and digital distributors into the music industry has provided the opportunity for more artists to release their music to broader audiences than ever before, the relationship dynamic between independent artists and aggregators has not been wholly symbiotic. From abrupt changes to pricing and subscription models, to a lack of clarity and assistance in problem resolution (Kabia), the laundry list of barriers to free navigation still remain extensive for independent artists seeking to use aggregators as their sole distributors. Many of the most popular aggregators like Distrokid, UnitedMasters, and TuneCore now use subscription models as opposed to the pay-per-release model which used to be industry standard, allowing for artists to release an unlimited amount of uploads within a chosen time period.
