As a hip hop form the Mixtape holds several valuable but different meanings within Hip Hop and popular culture. For Many such as Adam Banks, the Mixtape represents a type of anthology. Something that collects and organizes disparate songs and music traditions, organizing them into a conversation about particular traditions and motifs. For others, such as S. Craig Watkins, the mixtape is a collection of hip hop artifacts designed to undermine and disrupt mainstream ideas about music distribution and artist autonomy. Watkins traces the origin of the mixtape to the early recording of live shows as a means of sharing unreleased music that bypassed radio and traditional music distribution methods. The artist 50 Cent gained extreme notoriety in the 2000s for his use of the mixtape genre to release full length mixtape albums as a way of avoiding industry practices aimed at blackballing him for his edgy and controversial rivalry with Murder Inc. and the Queens black market syndicate.
