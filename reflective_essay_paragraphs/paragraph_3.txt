Over the course of my research project I’ve gathered and researched a wealth of sources, some of which disagree with my initial conclusions, some that agree, and even more that altered my perspective and have given me a more holistic understanding of the parties involved, conflicts of interest between them, and problem for which solutions are actively being worked on. For example prior to my research I wasn’t aware that one could create their own aggregator for a small cost in order to get their music of Digital Streaming Platforms (DSPs), but one thing I was aware of, is the increasing methods artists are using to sell direct to consumer in a digital era where physicals don’t hold as much weight. Insights such as these will likely have been the largest contributors to what eventually comprises my finished mixtape.
