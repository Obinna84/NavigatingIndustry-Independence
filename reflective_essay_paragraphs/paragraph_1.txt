Given there weren’t many guardrails surrounding what research topics I could venture and what questions I could formulate, aside from the specifications that it must deal with voice and technology, I chose a topic that was most interesting to me at the time. There are a plurality of reasons that exploring digital distribution and their relationship to independent artists came to mind first, but likely the largest was an interview I had seen a couple months ago between a popular YouTube music critic, theneedledrop, and a representative of the newly formed DistroKid union. While the demands of the workers didn’t directly relate to my research and the interview itself didn’t find it’s way into my overall research, the insight it provided in the background operation of DistroKid as a company remained in the back of my head as I would begin to start my research.
