In an effort to detail as much of my research and thought process as possible, the finished product of my mixtape will comprise four sections. The first section will contain my reflections and annotations, to detail my thought process throughout the project, how the sources I was working with were actively changing my holistic perspective, and even what biases I was going into writing my completed research papers with. The second section of my mixtape will comprise my essays, the one connected to this assignment, and the essay from the critical playlists project. This section is the most important part of my research as it will detail the final conclusions I drew from the entire process, and offer closure on the questions explored. The third section will comprise the interviews I conducted, with key parts being associated with time stamps. The final part of the mixtape will comprise all the images I think contribute to painting a full picture of the topics explored throughout the mixtape, and images that I like and wanted to include.
